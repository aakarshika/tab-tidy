# Code of Conduct

Tab Tidy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent and focus on what's best for the project and the people in it. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated in issues, pull requests or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@aakarshika](https://github.com/aakarshika), privately. Reports are kept confidential. You can also use GitHub's [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) tools.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.
